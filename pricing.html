Common questions

What's included in the monthly fee?

One 45-minute private lesson every week — four lessons per month. No extra materials fees; chord charts are provided at no cost.

Can we cancel any time?

Yes. No contracts, no minimum commitments. If you need to pause or stop, just let us know before the next billing cycle.

What happens if we miss a lesson?

Life happens. Give us 24 hours' notice and we'll reschedule at no charge. No complicated makeup policies.

Does my child need their own guitar?

A guitar is available to borrow for the intro session. We'll give honest advice on what to buy before you commit to anything.
